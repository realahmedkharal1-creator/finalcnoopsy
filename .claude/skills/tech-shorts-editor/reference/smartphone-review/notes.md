## What the four have in common

- **Order of topics**: hook (battery or a claim) -> display -> build/design -> name -> software
  updates -> cameras -> chipset -> rating or price. Price is always the last card.
- **One card per shot, 1.5-3.5 s each.** A card may span 2-4 quick shots of the same topic
  (chipset over multitasking shots, `Luminous Effect` over three demos).
- **Three text families**, chosen by backdrop:
  1. tinted glass card with 2-4 lines (bold value, light descriptor) for chipset, updates, protection;
  2. free text in a colour sampled from the scene for name, battery, display;
  3. glowing white callouts with thin lines for cameras.
- **Card tint follows the scene**: green over foliage, yellow over a warm wall, mint over grey,
  cream over wood, coral/pink for software features.
- **Placement**: top 12-30% of the frame on hand-held shots; on top-down shots text is tilted
  and placed along the product; lower third only when the subject fills the top.
- **Entrances**: typewriter/wipe left to right in 0.3-0.5 s for cards and lines; pop for word
  pills; highlight boxes wipe in 0.3 s about 1 s after the text.
- **Pacing**: average shot 2.1-2.5 s (1.0 s in the feature-list cut); every shot moves
  (push-in, orbit, slide or rack focus). Almost no talking head: 0-15% of the runtime.
- **Loudness**: -13.3 to -14.6 LUFS, one cut at -10.6.

## Card mapping in this skill

Available now: `pill` (single and stacked), `spec` (tinted glass card: set `top`/`bottom`),
`words`, `text`, `display`, `price`, `tag`, `product`, `sheet`, `subscribe`.
Not available: tilted or perspective text, camera callouts with lines, labels arranged around
a product, highlight box behind one word, highlighter sweep on screenshots, glow text,
speaker ripples, 3D icons, glitch titles.

# Techiebist: Samsung Galaxy S25 FE reel (split-screen reference)

Source: a public Instagram reel by Techiebist, 58.6 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Saved as
`techiebist_s25fe_split_screen_reel.mp4`. Frames: `frames/techiebist_split_1..5.png` are the user's phone
screenshots (they include the Instagram buttons and the phone crops the sides of the video, so measure from
the video, not from them); `frames/techiebist_*_clean.png` are clean full-size frames cut from the video.

Why the user wants it: the speaker stays on screen all the time (split screen), B-roll changes in the top window.

Measured: 29 cuts, average shot 2.0 s; no pause longer than 0.15 s; loudness -14.0 LUFS, loudness range
0.9 LU (very flat, heavily compressed). The audio was measured, not listened to; sound-effect types and music are unknown.

## The four layouts and how long each is used

| Layout | Time | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker full screen | 14 s | 24% |
| B-roll full screen + speaker in a circle | 20.5 s | 35% |
| **Split: B-roll window on top, speaker window below** | 22 s | 38% |
| Card: one portrait photo window + speaker circle below | 2 s (38.7-40.7 s) | 3% |

Order in this reel (seconds): speaker 0-2.7 | B-roll + circle 2.7-4 | speaker 4-8 | split 8-10 | speaker 10-11 |
B-roll + circle 11-14 | split 14-20 | speaker 20-21 | B-roll + circle 21-23 | split 23-29 | speaker 29-30 |
B-roll + circle 30-36 | split 36-38.7 | card 38.7-40.7 | split 40.7-41.5 | B-roll + circle 41.5-46.7 |
speaker 46.7-48 | split 48-51.7 | speaker 51.7-53 | B-roll + circle 53-57 | speaker 57-58.6 (follow card).
Rhythm: a speaker-only beat of 1-2 s always separates two B-roll blocks; no layout is held longer than 6 s.

## Split-screen geometry (1080 x 1920)

- Background: cream squared paper, about `#E9E0C7` with a faint grid, same texture in every paper layout.
- Two rounded windows, each **972 x 865 px** (about 9:8, almost square), centred horizontally:
  - left and right margin **54 px**
  - top window y 39 to 903, bottom window y 1005 to 1868
  - **gap between the windows about 102 px**, bottom margin about 52 px
  - corner radius about **50 px**; a very faint soft shadow
- Top window: B-roll, filled edge to edge (no blur copy, no bars), product in the middle of the window.
- Bottom window: the speaker (head in the upper-middle, shoulders cut by the window), continuous.
- Text that belongs to the B-roll sits **inside the top window**, bottom-left or bottom-centre, not on the paper.
- Cards that sit on the B-roll never cover the product: the phone is framed with space for the text.

## Card layout (38.7-40.7 s)

Same paper background. A single rounded portrait window (about 952 x 1260 px, x 84 to 1036, y 230 to 1488) holds a
tall photo (camera sample); the speaker circle sits below it, centred. Used for portrait samples.

## Speaker circle

- Diameter **394 px (36.5% of the width)**, white ring about **13 px** thick, soft shadow.
- Position: **bottom-right** for most B-roll blocks (about x 870, y 1690), **bottom-centre** (x 546, y 1692) for the
  first web-page card and the card layout.
- It stays on for the whole B-roll block and goes away when the speaker returns full screen.

## Full-screen B-roll with a browser/page card (2.7-4 s)

A web-page screenshot is placed as one rounded white card with a shadow on a purple-to-white gradient with fairy lights;
a red hand-drawn ellipse circles the offer price, a neon-green highlight sits behind "Buy at 39,999". Speaker circle bottom-centre.

## Text style

- Font: heavy geometric sans (Poppins / Montserrat Bold-ExtraBold look), mixed case.
- **Key value in yellow `#FFF200` with a yellow glow**, descriptor in white with a soft shadow, two or three lines,
  centred or bottom-left. Examples: `120FPS` (yellow) `BGMI` (white); `4900mAh` yellow over `45W charging` white;
  `4K video` yellow over `front & back` white; `6.7 FHD+` yellow over three white spec lines.
- Price in **neon green `#00FF00`** with glow (`39,999`), the old price in yellow with a red strike line.
- One accent in italic display serif: `premium` yellow italic over `design` white heavy.
- Number counter at the start: `33080` counts up to `60000` in yellow over the speaker, then the old price is struck out.
- Brand tag top-left on every frame: small white `Techiebist` with a thin red vertical bar to its left (about x 45, y 48).
- Last 1.5 s: follow card (Instagram icon, handle, "Following" pill) over the speaker.
- Currency: the reel uses the rupee sign. For this user write **Rs** instead.

## Transitions

- Into a B-roll block from the speaker: a **warm flash**, about 0.35 s (picture brightens and warms, fades to near white, next shot fades up).
- Between a full-screen B-roll and a split: the old layout **blurs for about 0.3 s**, the new layout then **blurs in**
  (windows start blurred, text stays sharp, paper background stays sharp) and clears over about 0.3 s.
- Every B-roll shot of 2-3 s has a slow push or drift; counters, stopwatches and temperature readouts are used as visual proof.

## Story pattern for a deal / review

Hook with the price drop (old price, strike, new price) -> "Worth it?" -> design (metal side, glass front) ->
display specs -> performance and gaming (120FPS) -> battery (4900mAh, 45W) -> heat and stopwatch tests ->
cameras -> software updates (7 years) -> "Available in best deal in Rs ..." -> follow card.

## What the skill can do now and what is missing

Already available: full-screen speaker, B-roll with speaker circle (position settable), edge-to-edge top/bottom halves,
yellow-glow style text is *not* available (cards use pills), product centring and headroom, price in Rs.

Not built yet (ask before building): the cream grid paper background; rounded windows with 54 px margins and a 102 px gap
(the current top/bottom halves are edge to edge); the card layout; circle bottom-right default, 394 px with white ring and shadow;
yellow-glow and neon-green text with white second line; the `Techiebist`-style brand tag; blur transitions; the warm flash;
the browser-card page and the red circle; the strike-through price counter; the follow card.
