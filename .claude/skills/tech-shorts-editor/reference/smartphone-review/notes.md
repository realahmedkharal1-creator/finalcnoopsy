## What the four have in common

- **Order of topics**: hook (battery or a claim) -> display -> build/design -> name -> software
  updates -> cameras -> chipset -> rating or price. Price is always the last card.
- **One card per shot, 1.5-3.5 s each.** A card may span 2-4 quick shots of the same topic
  (chipset over multitasking shots, `Luminous Effect` over three demos).
- **Three text families**, chosen by backdrop:
  1. tinted glass card with 2-4 lines (bold value, light descriptor) for chipset, updates, protection;
  2. free text in a colour sampled from the scene for name, battery, display;
  3. glowing white callouts with thin lines for cameras.
- **Card tint follows the scene**: green over foliage, yellow over a warm wall, mint over grey,
  cream over wood, coral/pink for software features.
- **Placement**: top 12-30% of the frame on hand-held shots; on top-down shots text is tilted
  and placed along the product; lower third only when the subject fills the top.
- **Entrances**: typewriter/wipe left to right in 0.3-0.5 s for cards and lines; pop for word
  pills; highlight boxes wipe in 0.3 s about 1 s after the text.
- **Pacing**: average shot 2.1-2.5 s (1.0 s in the feature-list cut); every shot moves
  (push-in, orbit, slide or rack focus). Almost no talking head: 0-15% of the runtime.
- **Loudness**: -13.3 to -14.6 LUFS, one cut at -10.6.

## Card mapping in this skill

Available now: `pill` (single and stacked), `spec` (tinted glass card: set `top`/`bottom`),
`words`, `text`, `display`, `price`, `tag`, `product`, `sheet`, `subscribe`.
Not available: tilted or perspective text, camera callouts with lines, labels arranged around
a product, highlight box behind one word, highlighter sweep on screenshots, glow text,
speaker ripples, 3D icons, glitch titles.
