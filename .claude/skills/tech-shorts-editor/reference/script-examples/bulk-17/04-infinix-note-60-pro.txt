Design ki baat karein to iss ka glass back, rear par matrix display aur premium in hand feel isay box pack phones mein alag banata hai, aur heart rate sensor bhi maujood hai.
Front par one point five K display hai, jo Poco ke muqable mein thori underpowered lagi, lekin brightness acha hai aur one forty four hertz bhi milta hai.
Battery timing ek se dedh din ki hai, aur is price par wireless charging bhi milti hai, jo ab bohat kam phones mein dekhne ko milti hai.
Lekin camera mein ye phone peeche reh jata hai, kyunke telephoto lens nahi hai aur optical stabilization bhi bharosemand nahi, jabke Oppo Reno sixteen F ya Moto Edge seventy Fusion zyada behtar hain.
