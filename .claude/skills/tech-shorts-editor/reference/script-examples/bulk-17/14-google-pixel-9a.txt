Andar Google Tensor G four chipset aur eight gigabyte RAM hai, jo sixty F P S gaming ko theek chalata hai, lekin zyada heat hota hai aur Pakistan mein five G kaam nahi karta.
Camera iss ki sab se achi cheez hai, jis mein main sensor aur wide angle lens ke saath four K sixty F P S video milti hai, lekin telephoto lens maujood nahi hai.
