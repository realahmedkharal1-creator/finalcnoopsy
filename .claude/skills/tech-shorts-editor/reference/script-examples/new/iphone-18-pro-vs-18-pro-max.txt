Assalam-o-Alaikum dosto, aaj hum baat karne wale hain Apple iPhone eighteen Pro aur iPhone eighteen Pro Max ke muqable ki, jin ke darmiyan kam az kam hundred dollar ka farq hai.
Dono ka design aur build ek jaisa hai, lekin Pro Max thirty eight gram bhaari hai.
Pro ki six point three inch aur Pro Max ki six point nine inch ki screen hai, jabke brightness dono ki barabar hai.
Dono mein Apple A twenty Pro chipset hai, lekin lambi gaming mein, Pro Max ka bada heat sink throttling kam karta hai.
Pro Max ki battery test mein nineteen ghante chali, jabke Pro seventeen ghante se kam, aur Pro Max ki charging bhi fifty four watt hai, jo Pro ki forty five watt se tez hai.
Camera setup, photo aur video quality dono mein bilkul ek jaisi hai.
Agar aap ko badi screen aur lambi battery chahiye, to Pro Max ke liye extra paise dena theek hai, lekin chhota phone pasand ho, to Pro bhi bohat acha hai.
