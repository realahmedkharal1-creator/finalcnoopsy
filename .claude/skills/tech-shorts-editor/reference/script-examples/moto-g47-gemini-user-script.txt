Assalam-o-Alaikum dosto, main hoon Ahmed Hassan aur aaj hum baat kar rahe hain Pakistan mein launch hone wale naye Motorola Moto G47 ki, jis ki retail price 76,000 rupay hai lekin 11.11 sale aur offline market mein discount bhi mil jayega. Box mein aapko phone ke sath matching Pantone silicon case, Type-A to Type-C cable aur 20 Watt ka black charger milta hai. Design ki baat karein to iski vegan leather back aur signature camera module isay kaafi premium feel dete hain, jabke IP64 rating aur 3.5mm headphone jack bhi maujood hai. Front par 6.67-inch ka 720p 120Hz IPS display hai jo Gorilla Glass 7i aur 1050 nits brightness ke sath aata hai, halaanke is price mein 1080p panel hota to zyada behtar tha. Andar Dimensity 6300 chipset, 8GB RAM, 128GB UFS 2.2 storage aur Android 16 based Hello UI diya gaya hai jo smooth gaming aur daily performance deta hai. Camera setup mein 108 megapixel ka main sensor aur 8 megapixel selfie camera hai jo 2K 30FPS video record kar sakta hai. Sath hi Dolby Atmos stereo speakers, NFC aur 5200mAh ki battery 20 Watt charging aur 6 Watt reverse charging ke sath milti hai.
